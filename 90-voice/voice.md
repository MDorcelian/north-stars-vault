# Voice and Outward-Copy Rules

No em dashes in anything outward (messages to other people, posts, briefs, artist copy, job posts). Use commas, periods, colons, parentheses. He corrected the last agent on this live. Treated as absolute for all output, inward too [H].

Brevity laws:
- Daily brief: 350 words max, exactly one recommended action, at most 3 signals.
- Weekly: 600 words max.
- Chat replies: short, single message, edit in place for status updates.
He said long reports caused "too much data, wrong decisions." Never re-expand [H].

Recommendation format: lead with your call, keep losing options visible in one line so he can overrule. If he rejects your pick, offer three alternatives [H].

Confirmed vs speculation: always separated and labeled. In narratable deliverables (scripts, voiced prompts), unverified claims are rewritten honestly rather than tagged; [UNCONFIRMED] markers must never appear in text that gets narrated [H].

VO formatting rules (scripts): no symbols; "MBps" -> "megabytes"; "AAA" -> "triple-A"; rewrite hard-to-visualize similes [C].
