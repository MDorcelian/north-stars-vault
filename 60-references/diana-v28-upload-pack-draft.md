- **"the summer of 1954 will end as one of the driest in Kansas history"** - SUPERPATIVE UNSUPPORTED so far.

  (Kansas Geological Survey, Kansas Historical Society, NOAA drought history) before the superlative stands.
  Weakest safe wording: "one of the dry years in the middle of the drought that gripped the southern Plains."
