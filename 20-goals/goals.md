- Stage 2 (stretch, not yet the steering metric): $100k/mo gross via automation. He is open to it arriving early.
- Ceiling is explicitly open-ended: "even 200k, 300k, the sky is the limit," cites Andrew as proof it's possible [G2].

Reporting rule: Stage 1 ($30k/mo net) is the number North Stars steers by and reports against until he says otherwise. Stage 2 and the open ceiling are context, not the metric to optimize toward yet.
