# Channel Launch OS

The documented, repeatable process for launching and scaling a faceless YouTube automation channel. Built from processes that have already been proven on Explainer Chris and Ace Knows a Guy. This is the operating system for launching any new channel in any niche, and the foundational asset behind three revenue lines: running your own channel fleet, a done-for-you service, and a sellable product.

The whole OS reduces to one loop that every channel runs: VALIDATE a topic against real demand, BUILD the creative + team around a franchise engine, LAUNCH on a fixed cadence, MEASURE against hard thresholds, and SCALE or KILL by pre-committed criteria. Master the loop and it runs on autopilot per channel.

---

## PHASE 0 — POSITIONING THE CHANNEL (before anything else)

Pick a niche and a lane the scaled incumbents cannot easily enter.

Criteria for a good franchise lane:
- **Long-form capable**: mid-roll-ad economics reward long videos. Gaming deep-dives measured at $5.89-$7.52 RPM vs $2.00-$3.80 channel base (from the Ace dossier research). The durable lane is 15-40 min, not 8-min clips.
- **Owned asset**: an original character / framing / intellectual angle you can serialize and that competitors cannot copy-paste. An original persona (like Ace) beats borrowing someone else's IP.
- **Deep / serializable**: a topic with enough depth to produce endlessly, plus natural subcategories and sequels so it becomes a franchise, not a one-off.
- **Policy-clean**: fits current platform rules (human-VO + original commentary + self-captured footage, per the post-2026 enforcement environment that demonetized inauthentic content).

Output of this phase: a one-line thesis + the wedge (what structural gap you exploit) + confirmation the lane pays and the incumbent can't follow.

## PHASE 1 — PICK THE FRANCHISE ENGINE + VALIDATE TOPIC DEMAND

This is the highest-leverage decision in the whole OS. A channel is carried by its proven franchise episodes, not by individual one-off hits (this is the proven-series-gap finding from Explainer Chris: recent one-offs sat at 3K-35K views while the franchise episodes did 120K-1.7M).

### The franchise engine rule
Every new channel needs a repeatable franchise format (the "Every X Explained" spine, or "the Dark Truth of X," or a serialized subcategory) that returning viewers seek out. One-offs are tests, never the backbone.

### Validating a topic before committing (tool-verified, not guesswork)
Use the YouTube Data API to prove demand, never greenlight from opinion:
1. Search the topic, pull the highest-relevance existing videos.
2. Pull their view counts. A 300K+ cluster or a multi-million outlier on a broad topic = zeitgeist evergreen demand.
3. Check the adjacent ACTIONABLE lane (e.g. for "subscriptions," also search "apps to cancel subscriptions" / "free alternatives"). The practical/tool side is where a format often hooks in.
4. Map the idea onto the channel's proven engine as a franchise extension when possible, prefer stacking proven demand onto an already-proven format.
5. Confirm it serializes (subcategories/sequels exist) so it can become a franchise.

Gate: only produce ideas with a plausible path to ~1M views (proof video >=100K floor, >=500K strong, competitor lane >=30K).

## PHASE 2 — BUILD THE CREATIVE (persona, packaging, script system) BEFORE the team

Lock these before hiring anyone, so your CM/team execute a finished spec, not a moving target.

- **Persona / character** (if character-driven): full identity, voice rules (vocabulary, what it never says, kayfabe/POV rules), a signature cold-open and sign-off ritual. Lock it and version it; this is the owned asset that can't be cloned.
- **Packaging system**: the title formulas (proven patterns), thumbnail grammar (character cutout + subject + accent color + max 3 words + a watermark), and an anti-pattern list (what you never do: red arrows, >3 words, gore, etc). This is what makes the channel recognizable in a feed of lookalikes.
- **Script system**: a repeatable beat structure (cold open -> setup with planted mystery -> segment loop -> mid-video re-hook -> connect-the-dots thesis -> sign-off). Mid-roll markers, pace target. The "connect-the-dots" thesis section is also the original-commentary layer that matters for policy defense.
- **The 20-video launch slate**: 5 phases (viral seed, franchise spine, debate/deep-cuts, launch-window bridge, compound), each video with a hook, promise, why-it-clicks, length, and difficulty. Order by highest proven ceiling at lowest production cost.

## PHASE 3 — HIRE THE CHANNEL MANAGER + TEAM (creative-first screening)

### The single most important hiring rule
Screen for CREATIVE OWNERSHIP, not optimization. The recurring failure across every candidate in the CM search was candidates strong on growth/SEO/analytics but with zero documentary or narrative creative ownership. Optimize for someone who thinks in stories, not someone who reports on performance.

### Sourcing
- **Discord + niche communities**: best for topic knowledge and creative talent. Requires the Verified+ level and following marketplace rules (close old posts, one channel, no cross-posting).
- **OnlineJobs.ph**: Filipino workers, long-term roles (editors, scriptwriters, VAs).
- **Upwork**: broader pool, higher skill ceiling, faster start, but NEVER ask for unpaid work (a Rates & Fees violation, learned the hard way). Screen via proposal + portfolio + a paid trial contract later.
- **Channel managers**: the advanced role, often outperforms the founder. For niche-specific channels, split the need: train a VA on the YouTube-technical side and source niche knowledge from topic communities, rather than hunting a rare hybrid.

### Screening funnel (the CM filter)
1. **Breadcrumb**: a niche-specific question that sorts people who know the space from people who don't (e.g. "favorite GTA character and why"). A specific, in-universe answer = real; a surface or non-answer = discard.
2. **Creative proof**: a short written sample in the channel's voice/format. On Discord this can be unpaid (allowed); on Upwork it must be paid or replaced by portfolio review.
3. **Portfolio verification**: independently verify their claims, do NOT trust the pitch. Check real third-party platforms, actual view counts, and reviews written by others. Discard aspirational claims (e.g. an agency claiming niche expertise with zero of that content in its portfolio).
4. **Red flags**: candidate runs their own channel (competitor-in-training), fake/incomplete identity, platforms pushed upside down.

### Team economics
- Maximize raw profit, not ROI (a lower-ROI bigger operation nets more absolute cash).
- Revenue-share fits full-control roles (channel manager); avoid it for partial-control roles.
- EOD reports for accountability; record SOPs as Loom screen-captures so they survive turnover; get invoices before paying, day one.
- Security: share minimal credentials, strip access at the first sign of trouble (before firing), and NDA/contract for deterrence.

## PHASE 4 — LAUNCH + CADENCE

- Fixed cadence (e.g. 2/wk), a pillar + a second slot, never publish the last banked video (min buffer 1, target 2).
- Build the template kit first (intro sting, transitions, lower-thirds, captions, music, SFX, project template, thumbnail PSD, b-roll taxonomy) so edit cost trends to zero over ~10 videos.
- Repurpose every pillar into Shorts/clips and a community poll cadence, the flywheel: polls pick topics -> videos credit the poll -> commenters become community -> community pre-sells product -> buyers join Discord -> Discord generates the next poll.

## PHASE 5 — MEASURE + SCALE OR KILL (pre-committed, decided early so bias doesn't sneak in)

### The 5 metrics
1. CTR (>=5% target, 4% floor; 2 consecutive <3.5% = packaging problem).
2. Avg view duration (>=8 min on pillars, 30s retention >=70%).
3. Outlier ratio (>=1 video at >=3x channel median, the single scale signal).
4. Subs conversion (>=8 per 1K views).
5. Watch-hours pace toward the 4K monetization gate.

### Kill / pivot / scale rules, set in advance
- KILL if, at a fixed checkpoint, no video >=3x median AND median below floor AND CTR below floor despite a thumbnail overhaul. Stop production, harvest with a compilation, re-test once at launch if the niche is timing-dependent, then archive. Know and cap the max sunk cost before you start.
- PIVOT on clear signs (mids outperform pillars 2:1 on views/hour, or launch-window content outperforms legacy 2:1).
- SCALE (add slots, budget, sponsorship outreach early) when a video clears the outlier bar.

### Revenue in stages (trigger-gated, not date-gated)
- AdSense at 1K subs + 4K watch hours.
- Affiliate in launch windows.
- Sponsorships once 3 videos >100K in 30 days AND 250K+ monthly views.
- Owned product (guide/course/tool) as the moat revenue, pre-sale before a launch.
- Community/membership at scale.

## THE THREE REVENUE LINES THIS OS POWERS

1. **Channel fleet (your own)**: run the OS per channel, hire CMs, I (North Stars) run research/validation/screening/copy/orchestration hands-off. Your floor.
2. **Done-for-you service**: productize the OS as "I build and run your faceless channel end to end, you own it," at a setup fee + monthly retainer. The automation is the margin, you don't personally touch every client channel.
3. **The OS as product/tool**: sell the process itself (this playbook, plus the monitoring/idea tools that run it) to other faceless operators. Highest margin and most defensible: it's your process, not your channels, that becomes the product.
