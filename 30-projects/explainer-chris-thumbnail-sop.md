# Explainer Chris Thumbnail SOP — CORRECTED (08-28, from the actual Trello SOP card + Marvens' examples)

## The style (matches real EC thumbnails)
- WHITE background, colorful.
- GRID of colored circles, each with a **slightly thick BLACK OUTLINE**, each containing a **clean vector logo**.
- SUBTITLE under each circle in **Comic Sans, black, NO outline**, ONE LINE only (no line breaks/wrap).
- No title text in the thumbnail (YouTube titles are not in thumbs).
- No host/person, no arrows, no clickbait. (Person-first applies to WADE, NOT Explainer Chris.)

## The SUBJECT element (KEY — this is what makes a thumb "fit")
- Golden: **a clean smartphone (or the topic device) on the far LEFT of the thumbnail** for context — "phones → put phones," "PCs → put PCs." The earlier version that was JUST circles/logos looked "emptier" than the one WITH the clean phone on the left. That device is the differentiator.
- The grid of circles/logos fills the rest to the right.
- Avoid ONLY-logos UNLESS the topic is software (logos ARE the differentiator there).

## Grid / layout rules
- Side-by-side (4-7 items) when logos are long.
- 4x2 or 5x2 (best for circles/squares). 4x3 if the images are 16:9.
- Even, uniform circles, equal spacing.

## LOGO SELECTION principle (IMPORTANT)
- Prefer ICON-ONLY vector logos (clean, no text) as the cell's visual.
- AVOID logos that contain text words duplicating the subtitle — e.g. Motorola "M" logo + subtitle "TURBOPOWER": good (doing okay: the "M" is icon-only). But a logo that READS the same word as the subtitle (e.g. a "Supercharge" logo + subtitle "Supercharge", Flashcharge, Huawei Supercharge-in-logo) is redundant/clashy.
- The LOGO should be the clean visual; the SUBTITLE carries the name. Don't repeat the word in both.
- Shorten subtitle labels to stay ONE line (e.g. "HUAWEI SUPERCHARGE" / "SAMSUNG SFC" wrapped to 2 lines = BUG; use shorter single-line labels).
