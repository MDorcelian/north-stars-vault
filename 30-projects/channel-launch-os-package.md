# Channel Launch OS — Sellable Package (pricing model v1)

This turns the Channel Launch OS into revenue. Two sellable forms, both anchored to the goal staging ($30k/mo net steering metric, $100k/mo gross stretch).

DISCLOSURE: the pricing below is a PROPOSAL I built, not verified market-comparable figures. Treat all numbers here as G3 (my proposed model) until confirmed against what comparable done-for-you / tool offers actually charge. The unit economics math is the reasoning part; the absolute price points need market validation before use.

---

## Offer A — Done-For-You (DFY): "I build and run your faceless channel, you own it"
Target buyer: someone with money but no band-with/time to build a channel (a working founder, a business owner who wants a new revenue line). They want a working channel, not another course.

### Packages
1. **Launch** (one-time): $4,000-$6,000. Channel positioned, persona + packaging + 20-video slate built (the OS Phases 0-2), first 4-6 videos produced, channel structured and set up. Client owns the channel + content.
2. **Growth retainer** (monthly): $1,000-$2,000/mo. OS Phases 3-5 run for them: research + topic validation weekly, pipeline orchestration (idea -> script -> edit -> thumbnail -> publish), candidate/team management if they want to hand off, performance monitoring + kill/scale decisions guided by the OS metrics. Minimum 3-month term. Includes my automation (research, screening, orchestration) as the margin, so client cost is mostly their production spend, my brain-power is the retainer.

### Why it's defensible
Most DFY sellers in the niche have never actually done it; this is built on a documented, results-backed process (Explainer Chris 52K subs / 1.7M-view franchise, the OS assembled from real wins). Proof beats PowerPoint.

### Unit economics (the reasoning part)
- At launch $5K avg + 6 clients/mo = $30k/mo gross (matches the stretch goal with room). Realistically: 2-3 launches/mo + 8-12 retainers gets there. The retainer is the compounding line, launch is the cash injection.
- My cost per client is near-zero incremental (automation), so margin is high, the constraint is actually finding enough paying clients and managing delivery quality, not per-client cost.

---

## Offer B — The OS as a Product (tool + playbook)
Target buyer: the faceless-operator market itself (people already doing this who want the process / the tools).

### Packages
1. **The Playbook** (digital product, on your existing Gumroad/Kit): $97-$197. The OS playbook redone to a buyer-neutral polished PDF (no resident-channel specifics), plus the checklist templates. Educational product, rides your existing Jiggy product infrastructure.
2. **The Online Tool** (SaaS, later): monthly sub, the research layer (topic-demand validation, competitor monitoring, idea scoring against the franchise-engine gate) exposed as a dashboard. $49-$99/mo. This is the highest-margin, most defensible, most scalable line, and it reuses the exact yt-dlp + YouTube Data API logic I already run.

### Why it's the ceiling
Selling the process/tool means revenue scales with subscribers, not your delivery hours. A $79/mo SaaS at 1,000 users = $79k/mo MRR with near-zero marginal cost. That's the truest path to the $100k/mo stretch without being head-of-team-of-20.
