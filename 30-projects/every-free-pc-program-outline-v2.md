### SECTION 1 — The Disk-Space Bomb (WIN 4.09M TAM) [lead]
Cold-open line: "Your hard drive isn't full because you have too many files. It's full because no one ever showed you what's actually eating it."
Programs: WinDirStat / TreeSize (visualize what eats space), plus practical "the bloat that fills SSDs" beat.
Why lead: biggest verified TAM in the whole idea (Chad Reddings 4.09M, PrimeGuide 1.88M). This is the hook that earns the click.
Gap vs existing: hardware videos underperform, outcome framing is under-served.

### SECTION 2 — The Forgotten Tool for a New PC (Rufus 870K)
Cold-open: "The first thing you should do with any new PC isn't install anything. It's making the thing you're about to clone onto it."
Programs: Rufus (bootable USB creation), contrast with BalenaEtcher (mention).
Why: 870K + 860K demand. Huge draw, desktop-only, no mobile equivalent.

### SECTION 3 — The Microsoft Tools Hidden in Plain Sight (PowerToys 287K)
Cold-open: "Microsoft ships you a toolbox to fix Windows, and most people will never know it exists."
Programs: PowerToys (FancyZones, PowerRename, PowerToys Run).
Why: ThioJoe 287K, Techno Tim 207K. Power-user win, high retention.

### SECTION 4 — The Screenshot Tool Nobody Replaces (Greenshot 191K)
Cold-open: "The print screen key is from 1995, and your Windows laptop still expects you to use the literal keyboard shortcut from 1995."
Programs: Greenshot, contrast with ShareX mention.
Why: Brett In Tech 191K. Low-overhead, universally useful.

### SECTION 5 — The Macro Machine (AutoHotkey 162K)
Cold-open: "There's a free program that can type, click, rename, and automate anything you do more than twice, and it's been free for 20 years."
Programs: AutoHotkey (macros/automation).
Why: 162K. Differentiator, desktop-only, high "oh wow" value.

### SECTION 6 — The Toolbox Microsoft Forgot To Ship (Sysinternals 105K)
Cold-open: "Somewhere on this PC, a process is running that you never started, and Windows Task Manager won't tell you what it is."
Programs: Sysinternals Suite (Process Explorer, Autoruns, TCPView).
Why: 105K (Prof. K). Kills the "PC cleaner" scam narrative, trust-building.

### SECTION 7 — The Second Opinion Antivirus (Malwarebytes 95K)
Cold-open: "Windows Defender is now good enough that you don't need a paid antivirus. But there's ONE free tool worth adding anyway."
Programs: Malwarebytes (on-demand supplement to Defender).
Why: 95K + independent-review angle. Directly counters paid-AV sponsor reads.

### SECTION 8 — The Office Suite You've Never Heard Of (OnlyOffice 72K)
Cold-open: "Everyone recommends LibreOffice. There's a second free office suite that looks closer to the Microsoft you're used to, and it's the one people don't mention."
Programs: OnlyOffice (distinct from already-covered LibreOffice).
Why: 72K + comparison framing (LibreOffice vs OnlyOffice is a comment engine).

### SECTION 9 — The Backup That Isn't Syncing (Duplicati 53K)
Cold-open: "There's a difference between syncing your files and actually backing them up, and most free guides skip it."
Programs: Duplicati (true versioned backup), contrast with Syncthing (already-covered, mention only as the sync non-answer).
Why: 53K, closes on "protect your data" (comment-driven, pre-empts the franchise sequel).
