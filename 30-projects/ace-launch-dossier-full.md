## 1. Positioning
Thesis: Ace, a corner-store insider narrating the GTA criminal underworld from inside it, owns the long-form (18-40 min) deep-lore lane. Target viewer: male 16-34, SA/IV-raised, wants Deep Lore (47% poll winner). Wedge: "THE 20-MINUTE INSIDER" — length economics (incumbents can't move to 25min without collapsing their model) + owned original character (not borrowed Rockstar IP).

## 2. Identity
Channel name: Ace Knows a Guy, @aceknowsaguy (locked, matches current vault). Tagline: "The GTA underworld, explained by the guy who knows everybody."

**Persona — full detail (supersedes prior vague vault note):**
- Early-30s corner-store owner, raised on the block, dodged the life. Access without culpability, in every room, on no paperwork. Everyone's friend, no one's soldier. Has nieces/nephews he's keeping out of the life, that's the warmth under the charm and the reason he narrates "why it goes wrong." Paid in favors, never money, running joke: "I don't get paid enough for this."
- Voice: fast, warm, conspiratorial. Code-switching, culturally fluent, never accent-as-costume. Plays dumb to set up the read, then lands it. High energy on hooks, drops low/close for reveals.
- Verbal kit: "word is...", "allegedly" (comedy beat + legal hygiene), "I know a guy", "you ain't heard this from me", "my guy", "pull up to the store", "let me get right for this one."
- POV rule (kayfabe): Ace lives INSIDE the GTA universe. Characters are people from his world, not game characters. Never says "in this game" or "Rockstar wrote" except as a deliberate wink in meta videos.
- Never says: real-world gang names or real criminal instruction, government names (only "a guy I know"), "in today's video", "smash that like button" (his version: "pull up to the store, it's free, unlike everything else in 2026"), who pays him, anything glorifying (thesis is "how it goes wrong"), punching down at victims.
- Cold-open signature: pulls cigarette from behind ear, "I know I should quit. Times are tough these days." (or rotating excuse variants) — lighter flick — "Aight. So." 6-10 seconds, never longer.
- Sign-off signature: one-sentence thesis recap + "You ain't heard this from me." + card flick + store bell SFX.

This is the REAL persona detail. Supersedes the earlier vault placeholder ("no deep lore, has one-liners, not fully locked"), that placeholder undersold how developed this actually is. It is not comic relief, but it does have personality/humor as a texture within a serious investigative frame ("allegedly" as both joke and legal hedge, playful deflections), not a jokester and not deadpan either.

Avatar: locked (orange beanie, ace-of-spades card, cigarette behind ear, black tank, gold chain, purple-navy gradient). Outstanding: card +15% resize, pendant = store key (default D4). Banner: Ace mid-story in front of corner store, golden hour, same illustrator pass as expression sheet (this is Vincent Bekong's work, see 40-people/vincent-bekong.md).

## 3. Packaging system
Title formulas F1-F7 (proven patterns): "Every X Explained", "The Dark Truth of X", "Hidden Signs of X You Never Noticed", "What Happened to X After Y", "X vs Y: Every Downgrade", "What Happens If You X", "Every X Ranked by Y".

Thumbnail grammar: Ace PNG cutout (1 of 5 canonical expressions) anchored left/right third + subject graded purple-navy on opposite third + orange-yellow accent + max 3 words + amber-key/teal-rim lighting + ace-of-spades card watermark in fixed corner on every thumbnail.

Anti-patterns (never): red arrows/circles, >3 words, gore/realistic-violence stills, lit cigarette in thumbnails/avatar, real gang imagery, leaked GTA 6 footage (DMCA risk), AI-photoreal faces, mimicking Explainer X's layout, all-caps word salad, shock-emoji faces on Ace (his brand is "knowing", eyebrow-raise is the ceiling expression).

## 5. Script system: "The Ace Format"
Repeatable 6-beat structure: (1) Cold open ritual 0:00-0:12, (2) Setup 0:12-1:15 with a planted mystery, (3) Segment loop per entity (claim/story/pivot/insider-detail/Ace's-read/transition), (4) Mid-video re-hook at ~40%, (5) Connect-the-dots thesis in final 2-3 min (the "original commentary" layer that matters for policy defense), (6) Sign-off. Mid-roll markers every ~4-4.5 min. Pace target 140-150 wpm VO.

**KILL checkpoint: Sep 15 (V14 live, 10+ videos, 2+ weeks data).** KILL if no video >=3x median AND median <12K views AND CTR <4% despite one thumbnail overhaul. Max sunk cost at kill: ~$6-8K (inferred, capped). PIVOT triggers and SCALE triggers also defined (see full source text if needed).

3. Take-Two/IP (leak coverage draws DMCAs). Mitigation: zero leaked footage ever, official/reported-news framing only, no Rockstar art in branding.
4. Operational fragility (editor/VO single points of failure). Mitigation: template kit as hiring tryout, dual VO casting, buffer discipline, 3h/wk cap on Marvens enforced by pipeline design.
