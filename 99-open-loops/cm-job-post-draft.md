# New CM Job Post (for Discord, drafted 2026-08-02)

Purpose: replace/lake the drained Discord 50k post. The prior post pulled growth-ops people, zero documentary storytellers (the recurring pattern). This version is re-tuned around the actual blocker: a creative channel manager who owns documentary narrative structure, not an ops/SEO/growth generalist. Modeled on Emanuele's hiring guidance (use samples/tests, screen by creative ownership) and the existing Screening SOP (breadcrumb -> GTA proof -> viral proof -> creative/ops split).

## Post (plain text, ready to copy)

Hiring a channel manager for a new GTA 6 documentary channel launching before the game drops in November. Brand is original character-driven deep-lore explainers, long form (18-40 min), not listicles, not news chasing.

I've screened a lot of applications and the honest pattern is that most are great at ops, SEO, growth, and analytics, which is not what's blocking me. I need someone who owns CREATIVE DIRECTION: story structure, narrative arc, how to turn deep-cut GTA lore into a compelling piece of documentary writing, not a bullet-point script.

What the role actually is, day to day: pick and shape video topics into a documentary angle, drive the script to a strong cold open / arc / payoff, oversee an editor and thumbnail direction, keep the voice consistent, own the content calendar through the Nov 19 GTA 6 launch.

You are NOT a fit if your background is purely optimization, growth, or analytics without demonstrated creative ownership of content. You don't need to be a lifelong GTA completionist, but you do need real GTA universe familiarity (deep lore matters here), or a proven ability to go deep fast.

To prove creative ownership, not just claim it, here's the ask: send me a 300-400 word outline for a video titled "Every GTA 6 Gang Explained," in the voice of a character who is an in-universe corner-store insider (thinks he knows a guy in every operation, talks like he's from that world, not like a game narrator). Cold open and sign-off should reflect a personality, not a generic documentary narration. This is unpaid but it's the filter I use to find the right person quickly.

If that sounds like you, DM me. Budget is $1,250/mo to start.
