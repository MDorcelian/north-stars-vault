# CM Job Post — guarded version v2 (drafted 2026-08-02)

Rationale: v1 revealed too much (the in-universe corner-store insider character, the "Every GTA 6 Gang Explained" test topic, the specific creative-ownership framing). Marvens wants a post that doesn't give away the channel's strategy; ability/creative fit is screened in DMs after they apply. This v2 keeps the job description real and the filter low-friction (a short written sample on a generic topic) without exposing the Ace character, the specific topic slate, or the documentary format details.

Compliant with marketplace rules: single channel, close old post, 24-hour spacing, no em dashes, no (1/2) numbering.

## Post (plain text, ready to copy into ONE channel)

Hiring a channel manager for a new gaming YouTube channel launching before a major game release this fall. Long-form content, character-driven, aiming for a strong position before launch.

Looking for someone who can own a channel end to end: topic selection, creative direction, script oversight, thumbnail direction, and coordination of an editor and voiceover across a consistent publishing cadence. This is a creative-first role, content direction matters more than raw optimization.

Note up front: I've seen a lot of applicants strong on growth, SEO, and analytics. That is not the gap I'm hiring for. I need someone with real creative ownership, someone who shapes what a video is about and how it's told, not just someone who reports on performance.

To keep this quick and fair for everyone: if you're interested, send me a written sample along with your intro. Writing a short outline for a video on a topic I'll give you. This is unpaid but it is how I find the right fit, and it applies to every applicant.

If you can show creative ownership and think in narrative, I want to hear from you. DM me. Budget is fair for the right person.
