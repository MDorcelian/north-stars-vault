type: system
status: active
vault: 50-systems

Input: a niche, competitor channels, channel analytics, and recent videos. [C:high] [S:file "OUTLIER SIGNALS by Jiggy"]
Step 1: identify outliers manually through YouTube search, through 1of10, or via NexLev. An outlier is a video that materially exceeds the channel’s normal view baseline. [C:high] [S:file "OUTLIER SIGNALS by Jiggy"]
Step 2: analyze packaging: thumbnail emotion/contrast/text/face, title promise, title structure, and whether the appeal is curiosity, outcome, fear, or relatability. [C:high] [S:file "OUTLIER SIGNALS by Jiggy"]
Step 3: analyze content: first-30-second hook, format, viewer problem/desire, cold-viewer appeal, trend versus evergreen signal, and whether newer/smaller channels also win. [C:high] [S:file "OUTLIER SIGNALS by Jiggy"]
Step 4: compare 3–5 outliers for recurring topic, format, thumbnail, title, length, and hook patterns. [C:high] [S:file "OUTLIER SIGNALS by Jiggy"]
Step 5: produce an original adjacent concept; outperform packaging; match or exceed depth; optimize metadata. [C:high] [S:file "OUTLIER SIGNALS by Jiggy"]
Step 6: identify internal outliers by comparing the last 90 days of top videos to the channel average; a video at 2x or more average is defined as an internal outlier. [C:high] [S:file "OUTLIER SIGNALS by Jiggy"]
Output: a validated content blueprint, not a copied title/thumbnail. [C:high] [S:file "OUTLIER SIGNALS by Jiggy"]
