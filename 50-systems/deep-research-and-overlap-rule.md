# Deep Research + Proof List Rule (Marvens' standing instruction)

## When triggered
Whenever Marvens says "research and add proof to the Trello card" (or any video-idea/'what to cover'
request), do DEEP research on YouTube AND the internet, and compile a PROOF list in itself, not just
paste a card name.

## What deep research means here
1. YouTube channel + video research (yt-dlp, Data API, evidence-brain outliers, niche_research.py):
   which TOPICS/ITEMS in the video are proven to pull views, with real numbers.
2. Internet/web research (crawl4ai, DuckDuckGo fallback): verify specific items are real, current,
   and that claims about them are accurate.
3. Compile the items with living proof (views, channels, sources), numerated, into the card desc.
4. ALWAYS check OVERLAP: cross-reference the proposed content against what the channel/its sister
   franchise ALREADY covered. Pull the actual covered list (e.g. the Free App 40-program list from
   the 4 scripts) and flag any item that's already done. Overlap in bulk = re-make, not remix
   (ideation-engine Gate 6). Separate/correct the list so the new item is genuinely new.

## Example applied (Free PC Program, 08-04)
The original Opus outline overlapped ~70% with the Free App franchise (VLC, GIMP, LibreOffice,
KeePassXC, OBS, ShareX, Syncthing etc were already covered). Corrected to a zero-overlap set
(Sysinternals, Ninite, Rufus, PowerToys, WinDirStat, Greenshot, AutoHotkey, OnlyOffice, Malwarebytes,
Duplicati) that is genuinely new desktop software. Lesson: never assume "desktop" is fresh, verify
against actual catalog.
