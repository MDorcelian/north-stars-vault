# Wanner Cash Cow coaching — durable takeaways (08-27 call, logged from transcript)

## Collaboration tool = spin-off launchpad (the big strategy)
How it works: publish on one channel, invite another channel as collaborator -> the ORIGINAL channel's video gets pushed into the COLLABORATOR channel's audience. For automation it's used as "big channel recommends a channel it also owns," not a real collab. Revenue is NOT split; it's an audience-push / recommendation.

- Safety: the collab tool does NOT link channels for strikes/demonetization (it is NOT one of the proxy/CMS/IP/device/AdSense linkages YouTube uses to tie channels to a user). Demonetized channels have used it to push new unaffected channels with zero blowback. Only real downside = audience + algorithm trust loss if over-used/relevant. It can slightly lower the big channel CTR if you push a mismatched audience.
- WHEN to spin off (the rule that matters): only once the main channel is medium/large in its niche (e.g. ~1M+ views/mo if top-dogs are 3M; or "a solid player"). A channel making ~100-200K views/mo is STILL TOO SMALL to push a child channel effectively. EXCEPTION: a brand-new niche YOU started with early momentum (100K outliers on a fresh concept) can justify an early spin-off. Otherwise if the main channel is small, hammer the MAIN channel to grow it first.
- The spin-off must overlap on AUDIENCE, not just topic (retiree-houses -> young-towns FAILS; same audience across different topics is best). Keep collabing while topics stay similar; stop once channels diverge/pivot away. Same packaging style + same voice = big boost (audience feels at home).
- Reverse collab (small pushes big) not useful; big channel already has that audience. No harm, just pointless unless the small channel outgrows the old one.
- Avoid abusing it, or YouTube may stop honoring your recommendations. It's an IGNITION / cold-start bypass, NOT a guarantee of success.
