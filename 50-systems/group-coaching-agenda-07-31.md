# Group Coaching Agenda: Hiring and Managing a Team (source outline)

Original slide/agenda doc backing the 2026-07-31 call transcript. Full narrative version already in 50-systems/hiring-and-team-management.md; this is the compact source outline for quick reference.

## 1. Hiring goals and mentality
- Maximizing net profit (not ROI), while having time to keep scaling.
- Get access to skilled people to do things better than you could (scriptwriters, voice actors, video editors).
- Save your own time and delegate tasks (e.g. VA for uploads, community management).
- Scale further (additional editors, writers, VAs).
- Can be a combination (e.g. channel managers who end up outperforming you).

Example: channel earning $5K/mo with $800/mo team invested. Double investment to $1,600, earn $8K/mo. ROI is lower, but profit in your pocket is higher.

## 2. Hiring tips
- Use the appropriate platforms: OnlineJobs, Upwork, Fiverr, Discords and specific communities.
- Look for smart and hard-working people, not just hard skills.
- Careful with overly ambitious people in more repetitive roles.
- Use tests and samples to cut through most candidates.
- Consider hiring full/part-time vs gigs.

## 3. Team management: Efficiency
- Get a good idea of the workflow and the timing of things.
- EOD reports.
- Time tracking can be a waste of time and lower morale, but still track productivity weekly.
- Provide references of competitors or make them research themselves.
- SOPs are key: record with Loom, make reusable rather than retraining people each time.
- Create a workflow appropriate for your current team size and complexity (tools and procedures).

## 4. Team management: Human aspect
- Check in on your team's personal situation.
- Share wins and praise the positive things.
- Project confidence and a sense of growth, make them feel part of something bigger.
- Do not get overtly friendly unless you have total trust in the person.

## 5. Team management: Contracts and security
- Limit your security risks in case of people quitting or getting fired: software access, assets, channel access.
- Contracts rarely get enforced, but still put something in place to clarify how things go.
- Get invoiced, check your country's laws.
