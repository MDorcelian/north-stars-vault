# Faceless Discord Marketplace Rules (for all future ads)

Verified+ required to post (earn XP, reach LEVEL 5) in marketplace channels, no exceptions.
No self-promotion or advertising in regular chat channels. No skill/trade/product in username.
Posts must be placed 24 hours apart, enforced by bot.
YouTube-related ads only.
Read each channel's description for category-specific requirements.
DO NOT cross-post the same ad in multiple channels (multiple posts removed). Pick the single most relevant channel. Different services = separate ads in applicable channels, or one agency ad.
CLOSE your existing post when posting a new ad or replacing it with a fresh thread.
No products/services against YouTube TOS (including botting).
Faceless not responsible for ads or transactions; issues handled off-server or via mod-help-ticket.

## Applied to the CM job post (08-02)
- Post is a YouTube-related hiring ad, allowed in marketplace.
- Must go in ONE channel only (single best-fit hiring/marketplace channel), not cross-posted.
- Close/delete the old CM post and post the fresh thread (rule says close when replacing). Do NOT run both.
- Remember the 24-hour-spacing bot rule when scheduling any subsequent ad.
- No em-dashes and no (1/2)/(2/2) numbering in the Discord-sent message.
